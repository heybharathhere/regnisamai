1. What this app does

Regnisamai (Reverse Singer) records audio using your device's microphone, plays it back forwards or in reverse, and stores your last 5 recordings, name, avatar, theme choice, and settings order locally on your device.

2. Your data stays on your device

This app has no server. Nothing you record is uploaded anywhere. Everything is stored only in your browser's local storage, on your device.

3. Microphone access

Microphone access is requested only when you tap Record, and used only to capture that recording.
