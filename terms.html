Side B: the fine print

Regnisamai is a small, offline-first recording toy. There's no account, no server, and nothing you record ever leaves your device unless you choose to share it yourself.

What the app stores

Your name, chosen avatar, recent takes, settings order, and theme preference are stored locally in your browser's storage (localStorage). Nothing is uploaded, synced, or transmitted anywhere. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app removes it all.

Microphone access

The app asks for microphone permission only when you tap the record button. Audio is processed entirely on-device for playback and the reverse effect — it is never sent anywhere else.

Your recordings

You're responsible for what you record and for respecting the rights and privacy of anyone else captured in a recording. Don't use the app to record people without their knowledge where that isn't allowed where you live.

No warranty

This is a hobby-grade tool provided as-is, with no guarantee it's free of bugs or that a take will always sound the way you expect — tape decks were temperamental too.
